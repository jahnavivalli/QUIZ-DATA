WHAT DID WE LEARN?

The 17 respondents helped us uncover some pretty clear patterns, but their spending habits weren't exactly identical.

They differed in what they spent on, how much they spent, and how often they made impulse purchases.

But there was one thing that brought them together like a hive mind:

FOOD.

Apparently, some things are universal.
